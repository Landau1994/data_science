# 习题 ML067：线性高斯最大似然在数据坐标变换下的协变性

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **Exercise 16.17（★★）** Consider a linear-Gaussian latent-variable model having a latent space distribution $p(\mathbf z)=\mathcal N(\mathbf x|\mathbf0,\mathbf I)$ and a conditional distribution for the observed variable $p(\mathbf x|\mathbf z)=\mathcal N(\mathbf x|\mathbf W\mathbf z+\boldsymbol\mu,\boldsymbol\Phi)$ where $\boldsymbol\Phi$ is an arbitrary symmetric positive-definite noise covariance matrix. Now suppose that we make a non-singular linear transformation of the data variables $\mathbf x\to\mathbf A\mathbf x$, where $\mathbf A$ is a $D\times D$ matrix. If $\boldsymbol\mu_{\mathrm{ML}},\mathbf W_{\mathrm{ML}}$, and $\boldsymbol\Phi_{\mathrm{ML}}$ represent the maximum likelihood solution corresponding to the original un-transformed data, show that $\mathbf A\boldsymbol\mu_{\mathrm{ML}},\mathbf A\mathbf W_{\mathrm{ML}}$, and $\mathbf A\boldsymbol\Phi_{\mathrm{ML}}\mathbf A^T$ represent the corresponding maximum likelihood solution for the transformed data set. Finally, show that the form of the model is preserved in two cases: (i) $\mathbf A$ is a diagonal matrix and $\boldsymbol\Phi$ is a diagonal matrix. This corresponds to factor analysis. The transformed $\boldsymbol\Phi$ remains diagonal, and hence factor analysis is covariant under component-wise re-scaling of the data variables; (ii) $\mathbf A$ is orthogonal and $\boldsymbol\Phi$ is proportional to the unit matrix so that $\boldsymbol\Phi=\sigma^2\mathbf I$. This corresponds to probabilistic PCA. The transformed $\boldsymbol\Phi$ matrix remains proportional to the unit matrix, and hence probabilistic PCA is covariant under a rotation of the axes of the data space, as is the case for conventional PCA.

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> **原题16.17。** 在线性高斯潜变量模型中，标准高斯潜先验配上均值 $Wz+\mu$、任意正定噪声协方差 $\Phi$。对观测作可逆变换 $x\mapsto Ax$，证明原最大似然解映为 $(A\mu_{\mathrm{ML}},AW_{\mathrm{ML}},A\Phi_{\mathrm{ML}}A^T)$ 后仍为新数据的最大似然解。再分别证明：对角 $A$、对角 $\Phi$ 保持因子分析形式，故对逐分量缩放协变；正交 $A$、$\Phi=\sigma^2I$ 保持PPCA形式，故对数据坐标旋转协变。

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示 1
> 对每个样本密度换元出现 $|\det A|^{-1}$，总对数似然只改变与参数无关的常数。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **独立推导。** 将原题先验的自变量误印 $x$ 理解为 $z$。原模型 $x=Wz+\mu+\epsilon$，$z\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$、$\epsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,\Phi)$ 独立。令 $y=Ax$，则
> $$y=(AW)z+A\mu+A\epsilon,\quad A\epsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,A\Phi A^T).$$
> 因为 $A$ 可逆，$A\Phi A^T\succ0$，参数变换 $T_A:(\mu,W,\Phi)\mapsto(A\mu,AW,A\Phi A^T)$ 在完整模型参数空间为双射，逆为 $T_{A^{-1}}$。换元公式给
> $$\ell_Y(T_A\theta)=\sum_n\ln p_Y(Ax_n|T_A\theta)=\ell_X(\theta)-N\ln|\det A|.$$
> 因此若原似然有最大值，任意新参数都可回拉为原参数比较，$T_A\theta_{\mathrm{ML}}$ 必为新最大值；即便存在多解也逐个映射。若最大值不存在，仅有上确界，则同理映射极大化序列，不能凭此产生不存在的MLE。
>
> (i) $A=\operatorname{diag}(a_i)$、$a_i\ne0$，$\Phi=\operatorname{diag}(\phi_i)$，则 $A\Phi A^T=\operatorname{diag}(a_i^2\phi_i)$，仍正对角；因子分析族对缩放封闭。
>
> (ii) $A$ 正交，$\Phi=\sigma^2I$，则 $A\Phi A^T=\sigma^2AA^T=\sigma^2I$，PPCA族不变。一般非正交变换可能把各向同性噪声变成各向异性，因此PPCA不对任意线性坐标变换协变。
