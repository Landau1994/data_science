# 习题 ST328：中位数正态极限的三角阵列证明

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **10.22** This exercise will look at some of the details of Example 10.2.3.
>
> (a) Verify that if $n$ is odd then
> $$
> P\bigl(\sqrt n(M_n-\mu)\le a\bigr)=P\left(\frac{\sum_iY_i-np_n}{\sqrt{np_n(1-p_n)}}\ge\frac{(n+1)/2-np_n}{\sqrt{np_n(1-p_n)}}\right).
> $$
> (b) Verify that $p_n\to p=F(\mu)=1/2$ and
> $$
> \frac{(n+1)/2-np_n}{\sqrt{np_n(1-p_n)}}\to-2aF'(\mu)=-2af(\mu).
> $$
> (Hint: Establish that $[(n+1)/2-np_n]/\sqrt n$ is the limit form of a derivative.)
>
> (c) Explain how to go from the statement that
> $$
> P\bigl(\sqrt n(M_n-\mu)\le a\bigr)\to P(Z\ge-2af(\mu)),
> $$
> to the conclusion that $\sqrt n(M_n-\mu)$ is asymptotically normal with mean 0 and variance $1/[2f(\mu)]^2$.
>
> (Note that the CLT would only directly apply if $p_n$ did not depend on $n$. As it does, more work needs to be done to rigorously conclude limiting normality. When the work is done, the result is as expected.)

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> **原题10.22。** 本题考察例10.2.3的若干细节.
>
> (a)  验证若$n$为奇数，则
>
> $$
> P\bigl(\sqrt n(M_n-\mu)\leq a\bigr)=P\left(\frac{\sum_iY_i-np_n}{\sqrt{np_n(1-p_n)}}\geq\frac{(n+1)/2-np_n}{\sqrt{np_n(1-p_n)}}\right).
> $$
>
> (b)  验证$p_n\to p=F(\mu)=1/2$，且
>
> $$
> \frac{(n+1)/2-np_n}{\sqrt{np_n(1-p_n)}}\longrightarrow-2aF'(\mu)=-2af(\mu).
> $$
>
> （提示：证明$[(n+1)/2-np_n]/\sqrt n$是导数的极限形式.）
>
> (c)  解释如何从
>
> $$
> P\bigl(\sqrt n(M_n-\mu)\leq a\bigr)\longrightarrow P(Z\geq-2af(\mu))
> $$
>
> 得到$\sqrt n(M_n-\mu)$渐近正态、均值为0、方差为$1/[2f(\mu)]^2$的结论.
>
> （注意，只有$p_n$不依赖$n$时，中心极限定理才可直接应用. 由于这里确实依赖$n$，要严格得出极限正态性还需更多工作. 完成这些工作后，结果与预期一致.）

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示
> 令Y_{ni}=I(X_i≤µ+a/√n)。它们每一行iid，行间分布变化，但有界性直接满足三角阵列CLT。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **证明范围：独立推导。** 假设$F(\mu)=1/2$，$F$在$\mu$可微且$0<f(\mu)<\infty$；$X_i$ iid连续，$n$沿奇数增大。令$Y_{ni}=I(X_i\le\mu+a/\sqrt n)$，$p_n=F(\mu+a/\sqrt n)$。
>
> **(a)** $M_n=X_{((n+1)/2)}$，$M_n\le\mu+a/\sqrt n$当且仅当至少$(n+1)/2$个观测不超过该阈值，即$\sum_iY_{ni}\ge(n+1)/2$。减去$np_n$并除以正标准差即原式。
>
> **(b)** 连续性给$p_n\to1/2$。可微性给$p_n=1/2+a f(\mu)/\sqrt n+o(n^{-1/2})$，故
> $$
> \frac{(n+1)/2-np_n}{\sqrt n}=\frac1{2\sqrt n}-a f(\mu)+o(1)\to-af(\mu),
> $$
> 分母还需除以$\sqrt{p_n(1-p_n)}\to1/2$，得到$-2af(\mu)$。
>
> **(c) 补足原注要求的工作。** 每行独立中心化变量$Y_{ni}-p_n$绝对值不超过1，总方差$s_n^2=np_n(1-p_n)\sim n/4\to\infty$。对任意$\epsilon>0$，充分大$n$时$\epsilon s_n>1$，Lindeberg尾和恒为0，所以三角阵列CLT给$\sum_i(Y_{ni}-p_n)/s_n\Rightarrow Z\sim N(0,1)$。阈值由(b)收敛且正态CDF连续，故右尾趋于$P(Z\ge-2af(\mu))=\Phi(2af(\mu))$。这正是$N(0,1/[4f(\mu)^2])$在$a$的CDF，完成非退化正态极限。若$f(\mu)=0$，该尺度与结论不再适用。
