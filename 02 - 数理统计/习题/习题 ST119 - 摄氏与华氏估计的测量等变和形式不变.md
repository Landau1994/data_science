# 习题 ST119：摄氏与华氏估计的测量等变和形式不变

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **6.39** Measurement equivariance requires the same inference for two equivalent data points: $\mathbf x$, measurements expressed in one scale, and $\mathbf y$, exactly the same measurements expressed in a different scale. Formal invariance, in the end, leads to a relationship between the inferences at two different data points in the same measurement scale.
>
> Suppose an experimenter wishes to estimate $\theta$, the mean boiling point of water, based on a single observation $X$, the boiling point measured in degrees Celsius. Because of the altitude and impurities in the water he decides to use the estimate $T(x)=.5x+.5(100)$. If the measurement scale is changed to degrees Fahrenheit, the experimenter would use $T^*(y)=.5y+.5(212)$ to estimate the mean boiling point expressed in degrees Fahrenheit.
>
> (a) The familiar relation between degrees Celsius and degrees Fahrenheit would lead us to convert Fahrenheit to Celsius using the transformation $\frac59(T^*(y)-32)$. Show that this procedure is measurement equivariant in that the same answer will be obtained for the same data. That is, $\frac59(T^*(y)-32)=T(x)$.
>
> (b) Formal invariance would require that $T(x)=T^*(x)$ for all $x$. Show that the estimators we have defined above do not satisfy this. So they are not equivariant in the sense of the Equivariance Principle.

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> **原题6.39。**
> 测量等变性要求对两个等价的数据点作出相同的推断：$\boldsymbol x$是在一种尺度下表示的测量值，$\boldsymbol y$是完全相同的测量在另一尺度下的表示.形式不变性最终则给出同一测量尺度下两个不同数据点处的推断之间的关系.
>
> 假设一位实验者希望根据一次观测$X$——用摄氏度测量的水的沸点——估计$\theta$，即水的平均沸点.考虑到海拔和水中的杂质，他决定采用估计$T(x)=.5x+.5(100)$.如果测量尺度改成华氏度，实验者会采用$T^*(y)=.5y+.5(212)$，估计以华氏度表示的平均沸点.
>
> (a)  摄氏度与华氏度之间熟知的关系使我们用变换$\frac59(T^*(y)-32)$把华氏度转为摄氏度.说明这个方法具有测量等变性，即相同数据将得到相同答案.也就是说，$\frac59(T^*(y)-32)=T(x)$.
>
> (b)  形式不变性要求对所有$x$均有$T(x)=T^*(x)$.说明上面定义的估计量不满足这一要求.因此，它们在等变性原则的意义下不等变.

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示 1
> 在(a)中代入$y=9x/5+32$；在(b)中比较相同数值的两个函数。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **证明范围：独立推导。** 教材输入与补充假设另行标明。
>
> **(a)** 同一测量的数值满足$y=9x/5+32$。故
> $$
> \frac59[T^*(y)-32]
> =\frac59\left[\frac12\left(\frac95x+32\right)+106-32\right]
> =\frac12x+50=T(x).
> $$
> 这里比较的是两个不同单位下的等价数值，并实际把估计结果也换回相同单位。
>
> **(b)** 若将同一个数值$x$直接放入两规则，
> $$
> T^*(x)-T(x)=106-50=56\ne0.
> $$
> 因此不具有所要求的形式不变性。两个比较并不矛盾：(a)的输入是满足换算关系的$x,y$，(b)的输入则是相同坐标数值$x$，不能把这两种要求混为一谈。
