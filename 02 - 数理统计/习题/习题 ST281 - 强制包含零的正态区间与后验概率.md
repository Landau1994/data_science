# 习题 ST281：强制包含零的正态区间与后验概率

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **9.33** Let $X\sim n(\mu,1)$ and consider the confidence interval,
> $$
> C_a(x)=\{\mu:\min\{0,(x-a)\}\le\mu\le\max\{0,(x+a)\}\}.
> $$
> (a) For $a=1.645$, prove that the coverage probability of $C_a(x)$ is exactly .95 for all $\mu$, with the exception of $\mu=0$, where the coverage probability is 1.
> (b) Now consider the so-called noninformative prior $\pi(\mu)=1$. Using this prior and again taking $a=1.645$, show that the posterior credible probability of $C_a(x)$ is exactly .90 for $-1.645\le x\le1.645$ and increases to .95 as $|x|\to\infty$.
> This type of interval arises in the problem of bioequivalence, where the objective is to decide if two treatments (different formulations of a drug, different delivery systems of a treatment) produce the same effect. The formulation of the problem results in “turning around” the roles of the null and alternative hypotheses (Exercise 8.47), resulting in some interesting statistics. See Berger and Hsu (1996) for a review of bioequivalence, and Brown, Casella, and Hwang (1995) for generalizations of the confidence set.

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> **原题9.33。**
> 设$X\sim\mathrm n(\mu,1)$，考虑置信区间
>
> $$
> C_a(x)=\{\mu:\min\{0,(x-a)\}\le\mu\le\max\{0,(x+a)\}\}.
> $$
>
> (a)  对$a=1.645$，证明除$\mu=0$时覆盖概率为$1$以外，$C_a(x)$的覆盖概率对所有$\mu$都恰为$.95$.
>
> (b)  现在考虑所谓无信息先验$\pi(\mu)=1$. 使用此先验并仍取$a=1.645$，证明$C_a(x)$的后验可信概率在$-1.645\le x\le1.645$时恰为$.90$，且当$|x|\to\infty$时上升至$.95$.
>
> 这类区间出现在生物等效性（bioequivalence）问题中，其目标是确定两种处理（药物的不同制剂、治疗的不同给药系统）是否产生相同效应. 这个问题的表述导致原假设与备择假设的角色“互换”（习题8.47），从而产生一些有趣的统计问题. 生物等效性的综述见Berger和Hsu（1996），置信集的推广见Brown、Casella和Hwang（1995）.

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示
> 覆盖概率按$\mu>0,\mu=0,\mu<0$分开；平坦先验的后验为$N(x,1)$，其区间质量再按$|x|\le a$和$|x|>a$计算。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **证明范围：独立推导。** 教材输入与补充假设另行标明。
>
> (a) 若$\mu>0$，下限总不超过0，所以覆盖等价于$X+a\ge\mu$，概率$\Phi(a)$；若$\mu<0$，覆盖等价于$X-a\le\mu$，概率同为$\Phi(a)$。$\mu=0$必被包含，概率1。
> (b) 平坦先验虽非正规，但后验为正规的$N(x,1)$。当$|x|\le a$，区间就是$[x-a,x+a]$，质量$2\Phi(a)-1$。当$x>a$，区间$[0,x+a]$的质量$\Phi(a)-\Phi(-x)=\Phi(a)+\Phi(x)-1$；$x<-a$对称，统一为$\Phi(a)+\Phi(|x|)-1$。它随$|x|$增加趋$\Phi(a)$。
> 严格数值上$\Phi(1.645)\ne.95$，原文的“exactly”采用四舍五入约定；若要精确$.95$和$.90$，应取$a=\Phi^{-1}(.95)$。上述概率公式精确，英文原题中的1.645与精确性声称均原样保留。
