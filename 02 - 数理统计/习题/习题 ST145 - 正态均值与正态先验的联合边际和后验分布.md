# 习题 ST145：正态均值与正态先验的联合边际和后验分布

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **7.22** This exercise will prove the assertions in Example 7.2.16, and more. Let $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ be a random sample from a $n(\theta,\sigma^2)$ population, and suppose that the prior distribution on $\theta$ is $n(\mu,\tau^2)$. Here we assume that $\sigma^2,\mu$, and $\tau^2$ are all known.
>
> (a) Find the joint pdf of $\bar X$ and $\theta$.
>
> (b) Show that $m(\bar x\mid\sigma^2,\mu,\tau^2)$, the marginal distribution of $\bar X$, is $n(\mu,(\sigma^2/n)+\tau^2)$.
>
> (c) Show that $\pi(\theta\mid\bar x,\sigma^2,\mu,\tau^2)$, the posterior distribution of $\theta$, is normal with mean and variance given by (7.2.10).

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> **原题7.22。** 本题将证明例7.2.16的断言，并作进一步推广.设$X_1,\ldots,X_n$为来自$n(\theta,\sigma^2)$总体的随机样本，$\theta$的先验分布为$n(\mu,\tau^2)$.这里假设$\sigma^2$、$\mu$和$\tau^2$都已知.
>
> (a)  求$\bar X$与$\theta$的联合pdf.
>
> (b)  说明$\bar X$的边缘分布$m(\bar x\mid\sigma^2,\mu,\tau^2)$为$n(\mu,(\sigma^2/n)+\tau^2)$.
>
> (c)  说明$\theta$的后验分布$\pi(\theta\mid\bar x,\sigma^2,\mu,\tau^2)$为正态，均值和方差由式7.2.10给出.

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示
> 写成先验乘正态样本均值密度，配方得到两个相互分离的平方项。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **证明范围：独立推导。** 设$v=\sigma^2/n>0$、$\tau^2>0$。
>
> **(a)** 条件于$\theta$，$\bar X\sim n(\theta,v)$。乘上先验得到
> $$
> f(z,\theta)=\frac1{2\pi\sqrt v\tau}
> \exp\left[-\frac{(z-\theta)^2}{2v}-\frac{(\theta-\mu)^2}{2\tau^2}\right].
> $$
> **(b)** 可表示$\bar X=\theta+\epsilon$，其中$\epsilon\sim n(0,v)$与先验$\theta\sim n(\mu,\tau^2)$独立。独立正态和的mgf相乘为$\exp[\mu t+(v+\tau^2)t^2/2]$，故$\bar X\sim n(\mu,v+\tau^2)$；这也给出边际密度的完整归一化常数$[2\pi(v+\tau^2)]^{-1/2}$。
>
> **(c)** 配方恒等式为
> $$
> \frac{(z-\theta)^2}{v}+\frac{(\theta-\mu)^2}{\tau^2}
> =\frac{(\theta-m)^2}{V}+\frac{(z-\mu)^2}{v+\tau^2},
> $$
> 其中$V=(v^{-1}+\tau^{-2})^{-1}=v\tau^2/(v+\tau^2)$，$m=V(z/v+\mu/\tau^2)=[\tau^2 z+v\mu]/(v+\tau^2)$。联合密度除以(b)边际密度，得完整后验$[2\pi V]^{-1/2}\exp[-(\theta-m)^2/(2V)]$。因此后验均值$m$、方差$V$，正是把例7.2.16单次观测公式中的观测方差替换为$\sigma^2/n$。
