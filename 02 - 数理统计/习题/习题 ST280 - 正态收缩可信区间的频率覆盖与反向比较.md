# 习题 ST280：正态收缩可信区间的频率覆盖与反向比较

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **9.32** In this exercise we will calculate the classical coverage probability of the HPD region in (9.2.23), that is, the coverage probability of the Bayes HPD region using the probability model $\bar X\sim n(\theta,\sigma^2/n)$.
> (a) Using the definitions given in Example 9.3.12, prove that
> $$
> P_\theta\left(|\theta-\delta^B(\bar X)|\le z_{\alpha/2}\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(\theta|\bar X)}\right)
> =P_\theta\left[-\sqrt{1+\gamma}z_{\alpha/2}+\frac{\gamma(\theta-\mu)}{\sigma/\sqrt n}\le Z\le\sqrt{1+\gamma}z_{\alpha/2}+\frac{\gamma(\theta-\mu)}{\sigma/\sqrt n}\right].
> $$
> (b) Show that the above set, although a $1-\alpha$ credible set, is not a $1-\alpha$ confidence set. (Fix $\theta\ne\mu$, let $\tau=\sigma/\sqrt n$, so that $\gamma=1$. Prove that as $\sigma^2/n\to0$ the above probability goes to zero.)
> (c) If $\theta=\mu$, however, prove that the coverage probability is bounded away from zero. Find the minimum and maximum of this coverage probability.
> (d) Now we will look at the other side. The usual $1-\alpha$ confidence set for $\theta$ is $\{\theta:|\theta-\bar x|\le z_{\alpha/2}\sigma/\sqrt n\}$. Show that the credible probability of this set is given by
> $$
> P_{\bar x}(|\theta-\bar x|\le z_{\alpha/2}\sigma/\sqrt n)
> =P_{\bar x}\left[-\sqrt{1+\gamma}z_{\alpha/2}+\frac{\gamma(\bar x-\mu)}{\sqrt{1+\gamma}\,\sigma/\sqrt n}\le Z\le\sqrt{1+\gamma}z_{\alpha/2}+\frac{\gamma(\bar x-\mu)}{\sqrt{1+\gamma}\,\sigma/\sqrt n}\right]
> $$
> and that this probability is not bounded away from zero. Hence, the $1-\alpha$ confidence set is not a $1-\alpha$ credible set.

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> **原题9.32。**
> 本题计算式9.2.23中HPD区域的经典覆盖概率，即在概率模型$\bar X\sim\mathrm n(\theta,\sigma^2/n)$下贝叶斯HPD区域的覆盖概率.
>
> (a)  利用例9.3.12中的定义，证明
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> &P_\theta\left(|\theta-\delta^{\mathrm B}(\bar X)|\le z_{\alpha/2}\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(\theta\mid\bar X)}\right)\\
> &=P_\theta\left[-\sqrt{1+\gamma}\,z_{\alpha/2}+\frac{\gamma(\theta-\mu)}{\sigma/\sqrt n}\le Z\le\sqrt{1+\gamma}\,z_{\alpha/2}+\frac{\gamma(\theta-\mu)}{\sigma/\sqrt n}\right].
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> (b)  证明上述集合虽然是$1-\alpha$可信集，却不是$1-\alpha$置信集.（固定$\theta\ne\mu$，令$\tau=\sigma/\sqrt n$，使$\gamma=1$. 证明当$\sigma^2/n\to0$时，上述概率趋于零.）
>
> (c)  不过，若$\theta=\mu$，证明覆盖概率有一个远离零的下界. 求此覆盖概率的最小值与最大值.
>
> (d)  现在来看另一面. $\theta$的通常$1-\alpha$置信集为$\{\theta:|\theta-\bar x|\le z_{\alpha/2}\sigma/\sqrt n\}$. 证明这个集合的可信概率为
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> &P_{\bar x}(|\theta-\bar x|\le z_{\alpha/2}\sigma/\sqrt n)\\
> &=P_{\bar x}\left[-\sqrt{1+\gamma}\,z_{\alpha/2}+\frac{\gamma(\bar x-\mu)}{\sqrt{1+\gamma}\,\sigma/\sqrt n}\le Z\le\sqrt{1+\gamma}\,z_{\alpha/2}+\frac{\gamma(\bar x-\mu)}{\sqrt{1+\gamma}\,\sigma/\sqrt n}\right],
> \end{aligned}
> $$
>
> 且此概率没有远离零的下界. 因而这个$1-\alpha$置信集不是$1-\alpha$可信集.

### 题内引用与条件

例9.2.18（印刷354–355／PDF385–386）给出后验及$\gamma=\sigma^2/(n\tau^2)$；例9.3.12（印刷363／PDF394）说明该正态可信区间为HPD。

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示
> 令$v=\sigma^2/n,\gamma=v/\tau^2$，先写收缩后验的均值和方差；(a)用抽样正态标准化，(d)改用后验正态标准化。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **证明范围：独立推导。** 教材输入与补充假设另行标明。
>
> 记$v=\sigma^2/n$，$\gamma=v/\tau^2$。后验均值$m=\delta^B(\bar x)=(\bar x+\gamma\mu)/(1+\gamma)$、后验方差$v/(1+\gamma)$。
> (a) 在重复抽样下$\bar X=\theta+\sqrt vZ$，$Z\sim N(0,1)$。于是$\theta-m=[\gamma(\theta-\mu)-\sqrt vZ]/(1+\gamma)$。将$|\theta-m|\le z\sqrt{v/(1+\gamma)}$乘$(1+\gamma)/\sqrt v$即原式，$z=z_{\alpha/2}$。
> (b) 当$\gamma=1$、$\theta\ne\mu$固定、$v\to0$时，两界的共同中心$(\theta-\mu)/\sqrt v$趋正或负无穷而宽度固定，正态概率趋0。此序列令$\tau^2=v$随抽样精度变化；若固定$\tau>0$再令$v\to0$，则$\gamma\to0$、偏移$\sqrt v(\theta-\mu)/\tau^2\to0$，覆盖反而趋$1-\alpha$。即使固定正$v,\tau$，令$|\theta-\mu|\to\infty$覆盖也趋0，足以否定全参数$1-\alpha$频率保证。
> (c) $\theta=\mu$时覆盖为$2\Phi(z\sqrt{1+\gamma})-1$。对$\gamma>0$，其下确界为$1-\alpha$（$\gamma\downarrow0$），上确界为1（$\gamma\to\infty$）；有限正$\gamma$时均不取到。故始终至少$1-\alpha$。
> (d) 现固定数据，在后验下$Z=(\theta-m)/\sqrt{v/(1+\gamma)}\sim N(0,1)$。经典区间转化为中心$\gamma(\bar x-\mu)/\sqrt{v(1+\gamma)}$、半宽$z\sqrt{1+\gamma}$的$Z$区间，正是原式。固定正超参数而令$|\bar x-\mu|\to\infty$，质量趋0。频率覆盖与后验质量属于不同概率空间，不能互推。
