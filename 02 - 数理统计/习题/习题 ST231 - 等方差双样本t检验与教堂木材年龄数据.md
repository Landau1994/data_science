# 习题 ST231：等方差双样本t检验与教堂木材年龄数据

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **8.41** Let $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ be a random sample from a n$(\mu_X,\sigma_X^2)$, and let $Y_1,\ldots,Y_m$ be an independent random sample from a n$(\mu_Y,\sigma_Y^2)$. We are interested in testing
> $$H_0:\mu_X=\mu_Y\quad\text{versus}\quad H_1:\mu_X\ne\mu_Y,$$
> with the assumption that $\sigma_X^2=\sigma_Y^2=\sigma^2$.
> (a) Derive the LRT for these hypotheses. Show that the LRT can be based on the statistic
> $$T=\frac{\bar X-\bar Y}{\sqrt{S_p^2(1/n+1/m)}},$$
> where
> $$S_p^2=\frac1{n+m-2}\left(\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\bar X)^2+\sum_{i=1}^m(Y_i-\bar Y)^2\right).$$
> (The quantity $S_p^2$ is sometimes referred to as a pooled variance estimate. This type of estimate will be used extensively in Section 11.2.)
> (b) Show that, under $H_0$, $T\sim t_{n+m-2}$. (This test is known as the two-sample $t$ test.)
> (c) Samples of wood were obtained from the core and periphery of a certain Byzantine church. The date of the wood was determined, giving the following data.
>
> | Core | Core | Periphery | Periphery |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | 1294 | 1251 | 1284 | 1274 |
> | 1279 | 1248 | 1272 | 1264 |
> | 1274 | 1240 | 1256 | 1256 |
> | 1264 | 1232 | 1254 | 1250 |
> | 1263 | 1220 | 1242 | |
> | 1254 | 1218 | | |
> | 1251 | 1210 | | |
>
> Use the two-sample $t$ test to determine if the mean age of the core is the same as the mean age of the periphery.

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> **原题8.41。**
> 设 $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ 是来自 $\mathrm n(\mu_X,\sigma_X^2)$ 的随机样本，$Y_1,\ldots,Y_m$ 是来自 $\mathrm n(\mu_Y,\sigma_Y^2)$ 的独立随机样本. 希望在 $\sigma_X^2=\sigma_Y^2=\sigma^2$ 的假设下检验
>
> $$
> H_0:\mu_X=\mu_Y\quad\text{对}\quad H_1:\mu_X\ne\mu_Y.
> $$
>
> (a)  推导似然比检验，并证明它可基于统计量
>
> $$
> T=\frac{\bar X-\bar Y}{\sqrt{S_p^2(1/n+1/m)}},
> $$
>
> 其中
>
> $$
> S_p^2=\frac1{n+m-2}\left(\sum_{i=1}^n(X_i-\bar X)^2+\sum_{i=1}^m(Y_i-\bar Y)^2\right).
> $$
>
> （$S_p^2$ 有时称为合并方差估计（pooled variance estimate），节11.2将广泛使用这种估计.）
>
> (b)  证明，在 $H_0$ 下，$T\sim t_{n+m-2}$. （这个检验称为双样本 $t$ 检验（two-sample $t$ test）.）
>
> (c)  从某座拜占庭教堂的核心和外围取得木材样本，测定年代得到以下数据：
>
>
> | 核心 | 核心 | 外围 | 外围 |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | 1294 | 1251 | 1284 | 1274 |
> | 1279 | 1248 | 1272 | 1264 |
> | 1274 | 1240 | 1256 | 1256 |
> | 1264 | 1232 | 1254 | 1250 |
> | 1263 | 1220 | 1242 | |
> | 1254 | 1218 | | |
> | 1251 | 1210 | | |
>
>
> 用双样本 $t$ 检验判断核心的平均年代是否与外围相同.

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示
> 约束均值为合并样本均值；原表每组两列都属于同一组样本。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **证明范围：独立推导。** 设$n,m\ge2$，$N=n+m,\nu=N-2$，$D=\bar X-\bar Y$，$Q=\sum(X_i-\bar X)^2+\sum(Y_i-\bar Y)^2=\nu S_p^2$。
> (a) 全MLE均值为各组均值，方差为$Q/N$。原假设下共同均值为$(n\bar X+m\bar Y)/N$，约束残差平方和为$Q+(nm/N)D^2$，共同方差MLE为它除以$N$。因此$\lambda=[Q/(Q+(nm/N)D^2)]^{N/2}=[1+T^2/\nu]^{-N/2}$，小比值等价大$|T|$。
> (b) 在原假设下$Z=D/[\sigma\sqrt{1/n+1/m}]\sim N(0,1)$，$V=Q/\sigma^2\sim\chi^2_\nu$，并由各正态样本均值与方差独立而相互独立。故$T=Z/\sqrt{V/\nu}\sim t_\nu$。大小$\alpha$时拒绝$|T|>t_{\nu,\alpha/2}$。
> (c) 按原表共14个Core值及9个Periphery值计算：$\bar x\approx1249.857143,\bar y\approx1261.333333$，$S_X^2\approx591.362637,S_Y^2=176$。合并$t\approx-1.290655525$，自由度21，双侧$p\approx.2108526894$。因此在$.05$水平不拒绝均值相等；这是没有充分反对证据，不是证明两个总体均值完全相等。数值由原表全部23项实际计算，未把两显示列误看成独立组。
