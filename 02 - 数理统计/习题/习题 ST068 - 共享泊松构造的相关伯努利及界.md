# 习题 ST068：共享泊松构造的相关伯努利及界

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **5.57** Park et al. (1996) describe a method for generating correlated binary variables based on the following scheme. Let $X_1,X_2,X_3$ be independent Poisson random variables with mean $\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3$, respectively, and create the random variables
> $$
> Y_1=X_1+X_3\text{ and }Y_2=X_2+X_3.
> $$
> (a) Show that $\operatorname{Cov}(Y_1,Y_2)=\lambda_3$.
>
> (b) Define $Z_i=I(Y_i=0)$ and $p_i=e^{-(\lambda_i+\lambda_3)}$. Show that $Z_i$ are Bernoulli$(p_i)$ with
> $$
> \operatorname{Corr}(Z_1,Z_2)=\frac{p_1p_2(e^{\lambda_3}-1)}{\sqrt{p_1(1-p_1)}\sqrt{p_2(1-p_2)}}.
> $$
> (c) Show that the correlation of $Z_1$ and $Z_2$ is not unrestricted in the range $[-1,1]$, but
> $$
> \operatorname{Corr}(Z_1,Z_2)\le\min\left\{\sqrt{\frac{p_2(1-p_1)}{p_1(1-p_2)}},\sqrt{\frac{p_1(1-p_2)}{p_2(1-p_1)}}\right\}.
> $$

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> **原题5.57。** Park等（1996）给出如下生成相关二元变量的方法.设$X_1,X_2,X_3$为均值分别为$\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_3$的独立Poisson随机变量，构造$Y_1=X_1+X_3$及$Y_2=X_2+X_3$.
>
> (a)  证明$\operatorname{Cov}(Y_1,Y_2)=\lambda_3$.
>
> (b)  定义$Z_i=I(Y_i=0)$、$p_i=e^{-(\lambda_i+\lambda_3)}$.证明$Z_i\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(p_i)$，且
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Corr}(Z_1,Z_2)=\frac{p_1p_2(e^{\lambda_3}-1)}{\sqrt{p_1(1-p_1)}\sqrt{p_2(1-p_2)}}.
> $$
>
> (c)  证明$Z_1,Z_2$的相关系数不能在$[-1,1]$中任意取值，而满足
>
> $$
> \operatorname{Corr}(Z_1,Z_2)\le\min\left\{\sqrt{\frac{p_2(1-p_1)}{p_1(1-p_2)}},\sqrt{\frac{p_1(1-p_2)}{p_2(1-p_1)}}\right\}.
> $$

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示
> 两个零指示同时为1等价于三个泊松变量全部为0。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **证明范围：独立推导。** 取$\lambda_j\ge0$。**(a)** 由协方差双线性及独立性，唯一非零项为$\operatorname{Var}X_3=\lambda_3$。
>
> **(b)** $Y_i\sim\operatorname{Poi}(\lambda_i+\lambda_3)$，故$P(Z_i=1)=p_i$。联合概率
> $$
> q=P(Z_1=Z_2=1)=P(X_1=X_2=X_3=0)=e^{-(\lambda_1+\lambda_2+\lambda_3)}=p_1p_2e^{\lambda_3}.
> $$
> 所以$\operatorname{Cov}(Z_1,Z_2)=q-p_1p_2=p_1p_2(e^{\lambda_3}-1)$，除以各Bernoulli标准差即原式，要求$0<p_i<1$，否则相关系数未定义。
>
> **(c)** 任意二元指示均满足$q\le\min(p_1,p_2)$。若用$q\le p_2$，协方差至多$p_2(1-p_1)$，标准化得第一个根式；用$q\le p_1$得第二个，取较小者即证。此外此构造$\lambda_3\ge0$使相关非负，无法生成一般负相关；给定边际还须$\lambda_3\le\min(-\log p_1,-\log p_2)$以保证$\lambda_1,\lambda_2\ge0$。
