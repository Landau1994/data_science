# 习题 DL323：分部积分消去未知数据得分

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **20.16 (★★★)** We cannot minimize the score function (20.43) directly because we do not know the functional form of the true data density $p(\mathbf x)$, and therefore we cannot write down an expression for the score function $\nabla_{\mathbf x}\ln p(\mathbf x)$. However, by using integration by parts (Hyvärinen, 2005), we can rewrite (20.43) in the form
> $$
> J(\mathbf w)=\int\left\{\nabla\!\cdot\mathbf s(\mathbf x,\mathbf w)+\frac12\|\mathbf s(\mathbf x,\mathbf w)\|^2\right\}p(\mathbf x)\,\mathrm d\mathbf x+\text{const}\tag{20.65}
> $$
> where the constant term is independent of the network parameters $\mathbf w$, and the divergence $\nabla\!\cdot\mathbf s(\mathbf x,\mathbf w)$ is defined by
> $$
> \nabla\!\cdot\mathbf s=\sum_{i=1}^D\frac{\partial s_i}{\partial x_i}=\sum_{i=1}^D\frac{\partial^2\ln p(\mathbf x)}{\partial x_i^2}\tag{20.66}
> $$
> in which $D$ is the dimensionality of $\mathbf x$. Derive the result (20.65) by first expanding the square in (20.43) and noting that the term involving $\|\mathbf s(\mathbf x,\mathbf w)\|^2$ already appears in (20.43) whereas the term involving $\|\mathbf s_D\|^2$ can be absorbed into the additive constant, where we have defined $\mathbf s_D=\nabla\ln p_D(\mathbf x)$. Now consider the formula
> $$
> \frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm dx}\{p(x)g(x)\}=\frac{\mathrm dp(x)}{\mathrm dx}g(x)+p(x)\frac{\mathrm dg(x)}{\mathrm dx}\tag{20.67}
> $$
> for the derivative of the product of two functions. Integrate both sides of this formula with respect to $x$ and rearrange to obtain the integration-by-parts formula:
> $$
> \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{\mathrm dp(x)}{\mathrm dx}g(x)\,\mathrm dx=-\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{\mathrm dg(x)}{\mathrm dx}p(x)\,\mathrm dx\tag{20.68}
> $$
> where we have assumed that $p(\infty)=p(-\infty)=0$. Apply this result together with the definition $\mathbf s_D=\nabla\ln p(\mathbf x)$ to the term involving $\mathbf s(\mathbf x,\mathbf w)^{\mathsf T}\mathbf s_D$ to complete the proof. Note that the evaluation of the second derivatives in (20.66) requires a separate backward propagation pass for each derivative and hence has an overall computational cost that grows quadratically with the dimensionality $D$ of the data space (Martens, Sutskever, and Swersky, 2012). This precludes the direct application of this loss function to spaces of high dimensionality, and so techniques such as sliced score matching (Song et al., 2019) have been developed to help address this inefficiency.

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> **原题20.16。** 由于未知真实数据密度 $p(\mathbf x)$ 的函数形式，不能直接求其得分并最小化(20.43)。使用分部积分将该损失改写为(20.65)，其中散度按(20.66)给出，常数项不依赖网络参数 $\mathbf w$。
>
> 先展开(20.43)的平方：保留网络得分范数平方项，并将 $\mathbf s_D=\nabla\ln p_D(\mathbf x)$ 的范数平方项吸收入常数。再对乘积求导公式(20.67)两侧积分，在 $p(\infty)=p(-\infty)=0$ 的假设下整理得到(20.68)。将该分部积分关系和 $\mathbf s_D=\nabla\ln p(\mathbf x)$ 应用于交叉项，完成(20.65)的证明。
>
> 计算(20.66)的各二阶导数需要分别反向传播，其总计算量随数据维数 $D$ 二次增长，因而难以直接用于高维空间；切片得分匹配（sliced score matching）等方法用于缓解这一计算问题。

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示
> 交叉项 $\int p\mathbf s^{\mathsf T}\nabla\ln p$ 等于 $\sum_i\int s_i\partial_i p$，移导数时产生负号。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **证明范围：独立推导。** 设数据密度 $p$ 正且足够光滑，$\mathbf s$ 可微，所需积分有限，且每个坐标的边界通量 $p(\mathbf x)s_i(\mathbf x,\mathbf w)$ 在无穷边界消失，满足Fubini与分部积分条件。展开原损失得
> $$
> J=\frac12\int p\|\mathbf s\|^2-\sum_i\int s_i\partial_i p+C,
> \qquad C=\frac12\int p\|\nabla\ln p\|^2.
> $$
> 对其他坐标固定，乘积求导并积分给
> $$
> \int s_i\partial_i p=[ps_i]_{-\infty}^{\infty}-\int p\partial_i s_i=-\int p\partial_i s_i.
> $$
> 代回即得 $J=\int p\{\tfrac12\|\mathbf s\|^2+\sum_i\partial_i s_i\}+C$，这就是(20.65)，且 $C$ 与网络参数无关。仅有 $p\to0$ 不足以保证 $ps_i\to0$，例如 $s_i$ 可能增长过快，必须检查乘积边界项；若数据仅在有界域上，应保留或处理该域边界通量。
>
> 原式(20.66)前半等号是散度定义，但末个等号只在 $\mathbf s=\nabla\ln p$ 时成立，不能把任意待训练向量网络的散度直接替换成未知真密度的二阶导数。若用势 $\phi$ 表示网络，散度应是 $\Delta\phi$。
>
> 朴素逐输出求Jacobian对角需约 $D$ 次反向传播，成本为 $O(D C)$；当网络单次成本本身按 $D$ 线性增长时才表现为 $O(D^2)$，不是所有架构的绝对律。随机迹估计或结构化方法可改变计算方案。本题解析恒等式本身不依赖采用哪种散度实现。
