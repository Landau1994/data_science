# 习题 DL236：二次误差上的早停特征方向滤波

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **9.6 (★★★)** Consider a quadratic error function of the form
> $$
> E=E_0+\frac12(\mathbf w-\mathbf w^\star)^{\mathsf T}\mathbf H(\mathbf w-\mathbf w^\star)\tag{9.56}
> $$
> where $\mathbf w^\star$ represents the minimum, and the Hessian matrix $\mathbf H$ is positive definite and constant. Suppose the initial weight vector $\mathbf w^{(0)}$ is chosen to be at the origin and is updated using simple gradient descent:
> $$
> \mathbf w^{(\tau)}=\mathbf w^{(\tau-1)}-\rho\nabla E\tag{9.57}
> $$
> where $\tau$ denotes the step number, and $\rho$ is the learning rate (which is assumed to be small). Show that, after $\tau$ steps, the components of the weight vector parallel to the eigenvectors of $\mathbf H$ can be written
> $$
> w_j^{(\tau)}=\{1-(1-\rho\eta_j)^\tau\}w_j^\star\tag{9.58}
> $$
> where $w_j=\mathbf w^{\mathsf T}\mathbf u_j$, and $\mathbf u_j$ and $\eta_j$ are the eigenvectors and eigenvalues of $\mathbf H$, respectively, defined by
> $$
> \mathbf H\mathbf u_j=\eta_j\mathbf u_j.\tag{9.59}
> $$
> Show that as $\tau\to\infty$, this gives $\mathbf w^{(\tau)}\to\mathbf w^\star$ as expected, provided $|1-\rho\eta_j|<1$. Now suppose that training is halted after a finite number $\tau$ of steps. Show that the components of the weight vector parallel to the eigenvectors of the Hessian satisfy
> $$
> w_j^{(\tau)}\simeq w_j^\star\quad\hbox{when }\eta_j\gg(\rho\tau)^{-1}\tag{9.60}
> $$
> $$
> |w_j^{(\tau)}|\ll|w_j^\star|\quad\hbox{when }\eta_j\ll(\rho\tau)^{-1}.\tag{9.61}
> $$
> This result shows that $(\rho\tau)^{-1}$ plays an analogous role to the regularization parameter $\lambda$ in weight decay.

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> 给定(9.56)的正定常Hessian二次误差，从零参数开始按学习率 $\rho$ 做梯度下降(9.57)。以Hessian特征向量为基，证明第 $\tau$ 步系数为(9.58)，当所有 $|1-\rho\eta_j|<1$ 时收敛至最小点。对有限早停步数，证明大曲率方向满足(9.60)、小曲率方向满足(9.61)，说明 $1/(\rho\tau)$ 类似权重衰减强度。

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示
> 对误差系数 $w_j^{(\tau)}-w_j^\star$ 写一阶递推。早停近似需要步长远离振荡稳定边界。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **证明范围：独立推导。**
> 实对称正定 $H$ 有正交归一特征基，$Hu_j=\eta_ju_j$、$\eta_j>0$。梯度为 $H(w-w^\star)$，故
> $$
> w_j^{(\tau)}-w_j^\star=(1-\rho\eta_j)(w_j^{(\tau-1)}-w_j^\star).
> $$
> 初值为0，归纳得 $w_j^{(\tau)}=[1-(1-\rho\eta_j)^\tau]w_j^\star$。若所有因子模小于1（等价 $0<\rho<2/\eta_{\max}$），有限维各坐标都收敛到 $w_j^\star$，即向量收敛。
>
> 解释(9.60)–(9.61)时令 $u=\rho\eta_j$，采用题设“小学习率”的非振荡范围 $0<u\le1$。若 $\tau u\gg1$，有 $0\le(1-u)^\tau\le e^{-\tau u}\ll1$，故 $w_j^{(\tau)}\simeq w_j^\star$。若 $\tau u\ll1$，Bernoulli不等式给 $0\le1-(1-u)^\tau\le\tau u\ll1$，故在 $w_j^\star\ne0$ 时所求绝对值比远小于1；$w_j^\star=0$ 时坐标恒为0，按精确等式理解而非严格大小比。
>
> 早停滤波因子为 $1-(1-\rho\eta_j)^\tau\approx1-e^{-\rho\tau\eta_j}$，高曲率方向已学到、低曲率方向仍被压缩。若改为二次权重罚项 $\lambda\|w\|^2/2$，最优系数为 $[\eta_j/(\eta_j+\lambda)]w_j^\star$，两种滤波的过渡曲率都在 $\eta_j\asymp\lambda\asymp1/(\rho\tau)$。它们不逐方向完全相等。仅有稳定条件而允许 $u$ 逼近2时，$|1-u|^\tau$ 仍可能很大；此时不能单凭 $\tau u\gg1$ 推出(9.60)。
