# 习题 DL289：双线性对抗优化的周期轨道

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **Exercise 17.2（★★★）** In this exercise we explore the problems that can arise from the adversarial nature of GAN training. Consider a cost function $E(a,b)=ab$ defined over two parameters $a$ and $b$, analogous to the parameters of a generative and discriminative network, respectively. Show that the point $a=0,b=0$ is a stationary point of the cost function. By considering the second derivatives along the lines $b=a$ and $b=-a$ show that the point $a=0,b=0$ is a saddle point. Now suppose that we optimize this error function by taking infinitesimal steps, so that the variables become functions of continuous time $a(t),b(t)$ defined by a continuous-time gradient descent, in which the parameter $a(t)$ of the generative network is updated so as to increase $E(a,b)$, whereas the parameter $b(t)$ is updated so as to decrease $E(a,b)$. Show that the evolution of the parameters is governed by the equations
> $$
> \frac{\mathrm da}{\mathrm dt}=\eta\frac{\partial E}{\partial a},\qquad
> \frac{\mathrm db}{\mathrm dt}=-\eta\frac{\partial E}{\partial b}.\tag{17.18}
> $$
> Hence, show that $a(t)$ satisfies the second-order differential equation
> $$
> \frac{\mathrm d^2a}{\mathrm dt^2}=-\eta^2a(t).\tag{17.19}
> $$
> Verify that the following expression is a solution of (17.19):
> $$
> a(t)=C\cos(\eta t)+D\sin(\eta t)\tag{17.20}
> $$
> where $C$ and $D$ are arbitrary constants. If the system is initialized at $t=0$ with the values $a=1,b=0$, find the values of $C$ and $D$ and hence show that the resulting values of $a(t)$ and $b(t)$ trace out a circle of unit radius in $a,b$ space centred on the origin, and that they therefore never converge to the saddle point.

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> **原题17.2。** 对代价函数 $E(a,b)=ab$，证明原点是驻点，比较直线 $b=a$、$b=-a$ 上的二阶导数证明它是鞍点（saddle point）。让生成器参数 $a$ 沿增加代价、判别器参数 $b$ 沿减少代价的方向作连续时间梯度更新，推导(17.18)，再推导(17.19)并验证通解(17.20)。初值为 $a(0)=1,b(0)=0$，求 $C,D$，证明轨道是绕原点的单位圆且不收敛到鞍点。三条公式完整列于英文原题。

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示 1
> 梯度为 $(b,a)$。沿两条指定直线，函数分别变为 $a^2$ 和 $-a^2$。

> [!hint]- 提示 2
> 由 $a'=\eta b$ 再求导，并用 $b'=-\eta a$。最后由 $b=a'/\eta$ 恢复另一坐标。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **证明范围：独立解析推导。** 设学习率 $\eta>0$。$\nabla E=(b,a)$，所以唯一驻点为 $(0,0)$。沿 $b=a$，$E=a^2$ 的二阶导数为2；沿 $b=-a$，$E=-a^2$ 的二阶导数为 $-2$。因此原点附近有更大和更小的函数值，是鞍点。
>
> 生成器上升、判别器下降的无穷小更新分别为
> $$
> a'=\eta\partial_aE=\eta b,\qquad b'=-\eta\partial_bE=-\eta a,
> $$
> 即(17.18)。继续求导有 $a''=\eta b'=-\eta^2a$，得到(17.19)。对(17.20)直接求两次导数即得 $a''=-\eta^2a$。同时
> $$
> b(t)=a'(t)/\eta=-C\sin(\eta t)+D\cos(\eta t).
> $$
> 初值要求 $C=a(0)=1$、$D=b(0)=0$，因此
> $$
> a(t)=\cos(\eta t),\qquad b(t)=-\sin(\eta t),\qquad a(t)^2+b(t)^2=1.
> $$
> 轨道永远与原点保持距离1，周期为 $2\pi/\eta$，所以不会趋于原点。这也可直接由守恒量验证：$\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm dt}(a^2+b^2)=2a\eta b-2b\eta a=0$。
>
> 该反例针对连续时间、同步上升—下降系统；不声称所有离散或带正则化的对抗算法都具有同一圆轨道。
