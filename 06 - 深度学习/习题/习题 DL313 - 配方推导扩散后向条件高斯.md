# 习题 DL313：配方推导扩散后向条件高斯

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **20.6 (★★★)** In this exercise we will use the technique of completing the square to derive the result (20.15) starting from Bayes’ theorem (20.13). First note that the two terms in the numerator on the right-hand side of (20.13), given by (20.4) and (20.6), both take the form of exponentials of quadratic functions of $\mathbf z_{t-1}$. The required distribution is therefore a Gaussian, and so we need only to find its mean and covariance. To do this, consider only the terms in the exponentials that depend on $\mathbf z_{t-1}$ and note that the product of two exponentials is the exponential of the sum of the two exponents. Gather together all the terms that are quadratic in $\mathbf z_{t-1}$ as well as those that are linear in $\mathbf z_{t-1}$ and then rearrange them in the form $(\mathbf z_{t-1}-\mathbf m_t)^{\mathsf T}S_t^{-1}(\mathbf z_{t-1}-\mathbf m_t)$. Then, by inspection, find expressions for $\mathbf m_t(\mathbf x,\mathbf z_t)$ and $S_t$. Note that additive terms that are independent of $\mathbf z_{t-1}$ can be ignored.

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> **原题20.6。** 从贝叶斯公式对 $\mathbf z_{t-1}$ 配方，求已知原数据的后向条件高斯均值和协方差(20.15)–(20.17)。

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示
> 精度为 $(1-\beta_t)/\beta_t+1/(1-\alpha_{t-1})$；一次项来自两个高斯。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **证明范围：独立推导。** 取 $t\ge2$，$\beta_t\in(0,1)$，记 $a=\sqrt{1-\beta_t}$、$r=\alpha_{t-1}$、$\mathbf u=\mathbf z_{t-1}$。分子负二倍对数核为
> $$
> Q(\mathbf u)=\frac{\|\mathbf z_t-a\mathbf u\|^2}{\beta_t}+\frac{\|\mathbf u-\sqrt r\mathbf x\|^2}{1-r}.
> $$
> 其二次系数 $c=a^2/\beta_t+1/(1-r)=(1-\alpha_t)/[\beta_t(1-r)]$，一次自然参数为 $\mathbf h=a\mathbf z_t/\beta_t+\sqrt r\mathbf x/(1-r)$。配方得到 $Q=c\|\mathbf u-c^{-1}\mathbf h\|^2+\text{常数}$，所以
> $$
> S_t=\frac{\beta_t(1-\alpha_{t-1})}{1-\alpha_t}I,
> $$
> $$
> \mathbf m_t=\frac{(1-\alpha_{t-1})\sqrt{1-\beta_t}\mathbf z_t+\sqrt{\alpha_{t-1}}\beta_t\mathbf x}{1-\alpha_t}.
> $$
> 这正是(20.15)–(20.17)。$t=1$ 时 $\alpha_0=1$，已知 $\mathbf x$ 后 $\mathbf z_0=\mathbf x$ 是点质量，不能按正协方差普通高斯处理；其极限均值为 $\mathbf x$、协方差为0。
