# 习题 DL298：一维连续流的概率质量与对数密度变化

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **Exercise 18.8（★★★）** In this exercise we give an informal derivation of (18.28) for one-dimensional distributions. Consider a distribution $q(z)$ at time $t$ that is transformed to a new distribution $p(x)$ at time $t+\delta t$ as a result of a transformation from $z$ to $x$. Also consider nearby values $z$ and $z+\Delta z$ along with corresponding values $x$ and $x+\Delta x$ as shown in Figure 18.7. First, write down an equation that expresses that the probability mass in the interval $\Delta z$ is the same as that in the interval $\Delta x$. Second, write down an equation that shows how the probability density changes in going from $t$ to $t+\delta t$, expressed in terms of the derivative $\mathrm dq(t)/\mathrm dt$. Third, write down an equation for $\Delta x$ in terms of $\Delta z$ by introducing the function $f(z)=\mathrm dz/\mathrm dt$. Finally, by combining these three equations and taking the limit $\delta t\to0$, show that
> $$\frac{\mathrm d}{\mathrm dt}\ln q(z)=-f'(z),\tag{18.39}$$
> which is the one-dimensional version of (18.28).
>
> ![[附件/习题图/DS003/原图-18.7.png]]

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> **原题18.8。** 参照图18.7，在一维速度场 $f(z)=\mathrm dz/\mathrm dt$ 下，将时刻 $t$ 的 $q(z)$ 变到 $t+\delta t$ 的 $p(x)$。依次写出：相邻区间的概率质量守恒、密度沿变换轨迹的时间展开、$\Delta x$ 与 $\Delta z$ 的关系。组合三式并取极限，证明(18.39)，即(18.28)的一维形式。

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示 1
> 使用 $p(x)\Delta x\simeq q(z)\Delta z$ 和 $\Delta x\simeq(1+\delta t f'(z))\Delta z$。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **独立局部推导。** 设速度场 $f$ 连续可微、流局部保持方向，沿轨迹密度为正。令 $Q(z,t)$ 表示带时间的密度，$q=Q(z,t)$，$p=Q(x,t+\delta t)$。所需三式在小区间与小时间展开为
> $$q\Delta z=p\Delta x+o(\Delta z),$$
> $$p=q+\delta t\frac{\mathrm Dq}{\mathrm Dt}+o(\delta t),\qquad\frac{\mathrm D}{\mathrm Dt}=\partial_t+f\partial_z,$$
> $$x=z+\delta t f(z)+o(\delta t),\quad\Delta x=[1+\delta t f'(z)+o(\delta t)]\Delta z+o(\Delta z).$$
> 先取小区间极限，再将后两式代入质量守恒，比较 $\delta t$ 系数得到
> $$\frac{\mathrm Dq}{\mathrm Dt}+qf'(z)=0,\qquad\frac{\mathrm D}{\mathrm Dt}\ln Q(z(t),t)=-f'(z(t)).$$
> 这就是(18.39)。正的速度导数使相邻轨迹分离、密度下降；负的导数使密度上升。关键是沿运动粒子的**全导数**，不是固定坐标的偏导数；Euler形式为 $\partial_tQ+\partial_z(Qf)=0$。多维中小体积伸缩率为 $\operatorname{tr}Df$，故对应(18.28)的 $\mathrm D\ln Q/\mathrm Dt=-\operatorname{div}f$。
