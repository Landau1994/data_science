# 习题 DL043：逻辑回归的损失梯度与热图

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **Problem 6.4\*** The logistic regression model uses a linear function to assign an input $x$ to one of two classes $y\in\{0,1\}$. For a 1D input and a 1D output, it has two parameters, $\phi_0$ and $\phi_1$, and is defined by:
> $$
> Pr(y=1\mid x)=\operatorname{sig}[\phi_0+\phi_1x],\tag{6.21}
> $$
> where $\operatorname{sig}[\bullet]$ is the logistic sigmoid function:
> $$
> \operatorname{sig}[z]=\frac{1}{1+\exp[-z]}.\tag{6.22}
> $$
> (i) Plot $y$ against $x$ for this model for different values of $\phi_0$ and $\phi_1$ and explain the qualitative meaning of each parameter. (ii) What is a suitable loss function for this model? (iii) Compute the derivatives of this loss function with respect to the parameters. (iv) Generate ten data points from a normal distribution with mean -1 and standard deviation 1 and assign them the label $y=0$. Generate another ten data points from a normal distribution with mean 1 and standard deviation 1 and assign these the label $y=1$. Plot the loss as a heatmap in terms of the two parameters $\phi_0$ and $\phi_1$. (v) Is this loss function convex? How could you prove this?

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> 对式(6.21)–(6.22)逻辑回归：(i)画不同截距/斜率的预测并解释参数；(ii)给损失；(iii)求梯度；(iv)从 $N(-1,1)$ 与 $N(1,1)$ 各生成10个样本，标签分别0/1，画参数损失热图；(v)证明或判断凸性。



## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示
> 用二元交叉熵，令 $v_i=(1,x_i)^T$，Hessian将是正权重的 $v_iv_i^T$ 之和。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **独立推导。** 令 $z_i=\phi_0+\phi_1x_i$、$p_i=\operatorname{sig}(z_i)$、$v_i=(1,x_i)^T$。预测应画概率而非把二元标签当连续值；截距决定 $x=0$ 的log-odds，$\phi_1$ 决定方向与过渡陡峭程度。非零斜率时 $p=1/2$ 的位置为 $-\phi_0/\phi_1$，零斜率则概率恒定。
>
> 负对数似然是
> $$
> L=\sum_i[\log(1+e^{z_i})-y_i z_i],
> \quad
> \nabla L=\sum_i(p_i-y_i)\begin{pmatrix}1\\x_i\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
> 由 $\operatorname{sig}'(z)=p(1-p)$，
> $$
> H=\sum_i p_i(1-p_i)v_i v_i^T.
> $$
> 任意 $u$ 有 $u^THu=\sum_i p_i(1-p_i)(u^Tv_i)^2\ge0$，所以凸；有限参数、输入至少两个不同值时正定。凸不等于总存在有限极小点，可分数据的极小值可能仅在参数无穷远处逼近。
>
> 本题已实际按所述两个正态分布各取10点，固定 NumPy PCG64 种子20261028；绘制四组参数曲线和 $[-4,4]\times[-2,6]$ 的161×161热图。图与数据、脚本共同保存，热图只展示网格范围，不作全局最优的计算证明。
