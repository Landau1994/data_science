# 习题 DL062：分叉计算图的反向微分

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **Problem 7.12\*** This problem explores computing derivatives on general acyclic computational graphs. Consider the function:
> $$
> y=\exp\!\left[\exp[x]+\exp[x]^2\right]+\sin\!\left[\exp[x]+\exp[x]^2\right].\tag{7.44}
> $$
> We can break this down into a series of intermediate computations so that:
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> f_1&=\exp[x]\\f_2&=f_1^2\\f_3&=f_1+f_2\\
> f_4&=\exp[f_3]\\f_5&=\sin[f_3]\\y&=f_4+f_5.\tag{7.45}
> \end{aligned}
> $$
> The associated computational graph is depicted in figure 7.9. Compute the derivative $\partial y/\partial x$ by reverse-mode differentiation. In other words, compute in order:
> $$
> \frac{\partial y}{\partial f_5},\frac{\partial y}{\partial f_4},
> \frac{\partial y}{\partial f_3},\frac{\partial y}{\partial f_2},
> \frac{\partial y}{\partial f_1}\ \text{and}\ \frac{\partial y}{\partial x},\tag{7.46}
> $$
> using the chain rule in each case to make use of the derivatives already computed.

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> 对式(7.44)及图7.9的有向无环计算图，按式(7.46)的指定顺序计算反向导数，每步复用先前导数。

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示
> 一个节点沿不同后继到达输出时，应把各路径贡献相加。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **独立推导。** 先按式(7.45)前向求各$f_j$，再逆序：
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> \frac{\partial y}{\partial f_5}&=1,\qquad
> \frac{\partial y}{\partial f_4}=1,\\
> A:=\frac{\partial y}{\partial f_3}
> &=\frac{\partial y}{\partial f_4}e^{f_3}
>  +\frac{\partial y}{\partial f_5}\cos f_3
> =e^{f_3}+\cos f_3,\\
> \frac{\partial y}{\partial f_2}&=A,\\
> \frac{\partial y}{\partial f_1}
> &=A+\frac{\partial y}{\partial f_2}\,2f_1
> =A(1+2f_1),\\
> \frac{\partial y}{\partial x}
> &=A(1+2f_1)e^x.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
> $f_3$的导数累加指数与正弦两支，$f_1$的导数累加直接到$f_3$及经$f_2$到$f_3$两支。因此结果是
> $$
> \left(e^{\,e^x+e^{2x}}+\cos(e^x+e^{2x})\right)(e^x+2e^{2x}).
> $$
