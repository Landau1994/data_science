# 习题 DL324：去噪得分匹配与边缘得分匹配等价

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **20.17 (★★)** In this exercise we show that the score function loss (20.50) is equivalent, up to an additive constant, to the form (20.49). To do this, first expand the square in (20.49) and by using (20.47) show that the term in $\mathbf s^{\mathsf T}\mathbf s$ from (20.49) is the same as the corresponding term obtained by expanding the square in (20.50). Next note that the term in $\|\nabla_{\mathbf z}\ln q\|^2$ in (20.49) is independent of $\mathbf w$ and likewise that the corresponding term in (20.50) is also independent of $\mathbf w$, and so these can be viewed as additive constants in the loss function and play no role in training. Finally, consider the cross-term in (20.49). By substituting for $q(\mathbf z)$ using (20.47), show that this is equal to the corresponding cross-term from (20.50). Hence, show that the two loss functions are equal up to an additive constant.

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> **原题20.17。** 证明去噪得分匹配（denoising score matching）损失与平滑边缘的得分匹配损失只差参数无关常数，分别核对平方项、交叉项与目标范数项。

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示
> 对边缘卷积求导，得到条件核得分在后验下的平均等于边缘得分。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **证明范围：独立推导。** 固定噪声尺度，联合分布为 $p(\mathbf x)q(\mathbf z\mid\mathbf x)$，边缘 $q(\mathbf z)=\int p(\mathbf x)q(\mathbf z\mid\mathbf x)\,\mathrm d\mathbf x$。记核得分 $\mathbf u=\nabla_{\mathbf z}\ln q(\mathbf z\mid\mathbf x)$、边缘得分 $\mathbf v=\nabla_{\mathbf z}\ln q(\mathbf z)$。在可交换积分与求导且边缘正的条件下，
> $$
> \mathrm E[\mathbf u\mid\mathbf z]=\frac{\int p(\mathbf x)\nabla_{\mathbf z}q(\mathbf z\mid\mathbf x)\,\mathrm d\mathbf x}{q(\mathbf z)}=\mathbf v.
> $$
> 两损失展开后的网络平方项都是 $\tfrac12\mathrm E_{q(\mathbf z)}\|\mathbf s(\mathbf z,\mathbf w)\|^2$。交叉项也相同，因为
> $$
> \mathrm E_{\mathbf x,\mathbf z}[\mathbf s^{\mathsf T}\mathbf u]=\mathrm E_{\mathbf z}[\mathbf s^{\mathsf T}\mathrm E(\mathbf u\mid\mathbf z)]=\mathrm E_{\mathbf z}[\mathbf s^{\mathsf T}\mathbf v].
> $$
> 剩余目标范数之差为 $\tfrac12\{\mathrm E\|\mathbf u\|^2-\mathrm E\|\mathbf v\|^2\}$，与 $\mathbf w$ 无关。更明确地，由条件方差分解，
> $$
> J_{\rm denoise}-J_{\rm marginal}=\tfrac12\mathrm E\|\mathbf u-\mathbf v\|^2\ge0.
> $$
> 所以二者具有相同参数梯度和最优解（在相应有限矩条件下），但数值一般不相等。此结论不需要预先知道真实数据密度的解析表达，只需能够从数据和已知噪声核抽样。
