# 习题 DL059：仿射权重梯度的外积形式

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **Problem 7.9\*** Consider a loss function $\ell[f]$, where $f=\beta+\Omega h$. We want to find how the loss $\ell$ changes when we change $\Omega$, which we’ll express with a matrix that contains the derivative $\partial\ell/\partial\Omega_{ij}$ at the $i^{th}$ row and $j^{th}$ column. Find an expression for $\partial f_i/\partial\Omega_{ij}$ and, using the chain rule, show that:
> $$
> \frac{\partial\ell}{\partial\Omega}=\frac{\partial\ell}{\partial f}h^T.\tag{7.42}
> $$

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> 对仿射层和标量损失，求单个输出对权重的导数，证明整个权重梯度是输出梯度与输入的外积。



## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示
> 改变Ωij只直接改变输出fi。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **独立推导。** 设$f\in\mathbb R^m$、$h\in\mathbb R^n$。由$f_k=\beta_k+\sum_l\Omega_{kl}h_l$可得
> $$
> \frac{\partial f_k}{\partial\Omega_{ij}}=\delta_{ki}h_j,
> \qquad \frac{\partial f_i}{\partial\Omega_{ij}}=h_j.
> $$
> 求本层参数导数时把本层输入$h$固定，链式法则给出
> $$
> \frac{\partial\ell}{\partial\Omega_{ij}}
> =\sum_{k=1}^m\frac{\partial\ell}{\partial f_k}
>  \frac{\partial f_k}{\partial\Omega_{ij}}
> =\frac{\partial\ell}{\partial f_i}h_j.
> $$
> 右侧恰为列向量$\partial\ell/\partial f\in\mathbb R^{m\times1}$与$h^T\in\mathbb R^{1\times n}$外积的$(i,j)$元素，所以得到式(7.42)。
