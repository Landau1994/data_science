# 习题 PM003：三元二值分布的边缘相关与条件独立

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **11.3 (★★)** Consider three binary variables $a,b,c\in\{0,1\}$ having the joint distribution given in Table 11.1. Show by direct evaluation that this distribution has the property that $a$ and $b$ are marginally dependent, so that $p(a,b)\ne p(a)p(b)$, but that they become independent when conditioned on $c$, so that $p(a,b\mid c)=p(a\mid c)p(b\mid c)$ for both $c=0$ and $c=1$.
>
> **Table 11.1** The joint distribution over three binary variables.
>
> | $a$ | $b$ | $c$ | $p(a,b,c)$ |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0.192 |
> | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0.144 |
> | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0.048 |
> | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0.216 |
> | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0.192 |
> | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0.064 |
> | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0.048 |
> | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0.096 |

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> 设三个二值变量$a,b,c\in\{0,1\}$具有表11.1中的联合分布。直接计算，证明$a,b$的边缘分布不独立，即$p(a,b)\ne p(a)p(b)$；但在给定$c=0$以及$c=1$时都条件独立，即$p(a,b\mid c)=p(a\mid c)p(b\mid c)$。
>
> 表11.1如下。
>
> | $a$ | $b$ | $c$ | $p(a,b,c)$ |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0.192 |
> | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0.144 |
> | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0.048 |
> | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0.216 |
> | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0.192 |
> | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0.064 |
> | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0.048 |
> | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0.096 |

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示 1
> 分别求和消去$c$，再将每个固定$c$的二维切片除以$p(c)$。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **独立推导。**
>
> 将二维表的行设为$a=0,1$，列设为$b=0,1$。边缘联合分布为
> $$
> P_{ab}=\begin{pmatrix}0.336&0.264\\0.256&0.144\end{pmatrix},\qquad
> P_a=(0.6,0.4),\quad P_b=(0.592,0.408).
> $$
> 其中$p(a=0,b=0)=0.336$，而$p(a=0)p(b=0)=0.6\times0.592=0.3552$，故不独立。
>
> 对$c=0$，$p(c=0)=0.48$，于是
> $$
> P_{ab\mid0}=\begin{pmatrix}0.4&0.1\\0.4&0.1\end{pmatrix}
> =\begin{pmatrix}1/2\\1/2\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}4/5&1/5\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
> 对$c=1$，$p(c=1)=0.52$，于是
> $$
> P_{ab\mid1}=\begin{pmatrix}18/65&27/65\\8/65&12/65\end{pmatrix}
> =\begin{pmatrix}9/13\\4/13\end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix}2/5&3/5\end{pmatrix}.
> $$
> 两张条件联合表都等于各自条件边缘的外积，因此两个条件值下均有$a\perp b\mid c$。
