# 习题 PM016：从联合分解验证一阶与二阶马尔可夫性

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **11.16 (★★)** Consider the joint probability distribution (11.44) corresponding to the directed graph of Figure 11.29. Using the sum and product rules of probability, verify that this joint distribution satisfies the conditional independence property (11.45) for $n=2,\ldots,N$. Similarly, show that the second-order Markov model described by the joint distribution (11.46) satisfies the conditional independence property
> $$
> p(x_n\mid x_1,\ldots,x_{n-1})=p(x_n\mid x_{n-1},x_{n-2})\tag{11.52}
> $$
> for $n=3,\ldots,N$.
>
> ![[附件/习题图/DS003/原图-11.29.png]]

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> 对于图11.29所对应的联合分布(11.44)，用概率求和规则与乘积规则验证：对$n=2,\ldots,N$，有(11.45)的一阶马尔可夫条件独立性。类似地，证明(11.46)的二阶模型对$n=3,\ldots,N$满足式(11.52)：
> $$
> p(x_n\mid x_1,\ldots,x_{n-1})=p(x_n\mid x_{n-1},x_{n-2}).
> $$

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示 1
> 从$x_N$逆序消去未来变量，再用相邻两个前缀联合密度作比值。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **独立推导。**
>
> 一阶模型为
> $$
> p(x_{1:N})=p(x_1)\prod_{j=2}^Np(x_j\mid x_{j-1}).
> $$
> 各条件分布归一化，依次对$x_N,\ldots,x_{n+1}$求和或积分，得
> $$
> p(x_{1:n})=p(x_1)\prod_{j=2}^np(x_j\mid x_{j-1}).
> $$
> 再除以$p(x_{1:n-1})$，公共因子消去，便有
> $$
> p(x_n\mid x_{1:n-1})=p(x_n\mid x_{n-1}),\qquad n\geq2.
> $$
> 二阶模型为
> $$
> p(x_{1:N})=p(x_1)p(x_2\mid x_1)\prod_{j=3}^Np(x_j\mid x_{j-1},x_{j-2}).
> $$
> 同样逆序消去未来得到截至$n$的前缀分解，再除以前缀$n-1$的分解，得到
> $$
> p(x_n\mid x_{1:n-1})=p(x_n\mid x_{n-1},x_{n-2}),\qquad n\geq3.
> $$
> 比值只在分母正的条件值上使用；一般表述为对历史分布几乎处处的条件概率等式。
