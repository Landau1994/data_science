# 习题 PM014：不可靠司机报告下的解释消除

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **11.14 (★★)** Consider the example of the car fuel system shown in Figure 11.20, and suppose that instead of observing the state of the fuel gauge $G$ directly, the gauge is seen by the driver $D$, who reports to us the reading on the gauge. This report says that the gauge shows either that the tank is full $D=1$ or that it is empty $D=0$. Our driver is a bit unreliable, as expressed through the following probabilities:
> $$
> p(D=1\mid G=1)=0.9\tag{11.50}
> $$
> $$
> p(D=0\mid G=0)=0.9.\tag{11.51}
> $$
> Suppose that the driver tells us that the fuel gauge shows empty, in other words that we observe $D=0$. Evaluate the probability that the tank is empty given only this observation. Similarly, evaluate the corresponding probability given also the observation that the battery is flat, and note that this second probability is lower. Discuss the intuition behind this result, and relate the result to Figure 11.32.
>
> ![[附件/习题图/DS003/原图-11.20.png]]
>
> ![[附件/习题图/DS003/原图-11.32.png]]

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> 考虑图11.20的汽车燃油系统。我们不直接观测油量表$G$，而由司机$D$查看并报告：$D=1$表示油量表显示满，$D=0$表示显示空。司机不完全可靠，$p(D=1\mid G=1)=0.9$、$p(D=0\mid G=0)=0.9$。仅观测到司机报告$D=0$时，求油箱为空的概率；再额外得知电池没电时，求对应概率并说明后者更低。解释其直觉，并联系图11.32。

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示 1
> 先消去$G$，计算$p(D=0\mid B,F)=0.1+0.8p(G=0\mid B,F)$。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **独立推导。**
>
> 沿用正文：$B,F$先验独立，$p(B=1)=p(F=1)=0.9$；按$(B,F)=(1,1),(1,0),(0,1),(0,0)$排列，$p(G=1\mid B,F)$为$(0.8,0.2,0.2,0.1)$。司机仅通过$G$依赖系统，故
> $$
> p(D=0\mid B,F)=0.9p(G=0\mid B,F)+0.1p(G=1\mid B,F),
> $$
> 相应四个值为$(0.26,0.74,0.74,0.82)$。边缘化可得
> $$
> p(D=0)=0.81(0.26)+0.09(0.74)+0.09(0.74)+0.01(0.82)=0.352,
> $$
> $$
> p(F=0,D=0)=0.09(0.74)+0.01(0.82)=0.0748.
> $$
> 所以
> $$
> p(F=0\mid D=0)=\frac{0.0748}{0.352}=0.2125.
> $$
> 若再给定$B=0$，
> $$
> p(F=0\mid D=0,B=0)
> =\frac{0.1(0.82)}{0.9(0.74)+0.1(0.82)}
> =\frac{0.082}{0.748}=\frac{41}{374}\approx0.109626.
> $$
> 于是第二个概率小于第一个，仍略高于先验$0.1$。司机报告空为油箱空提供证据；得知电池没电后，电池故障也能解释该报告，从而削弱对油箱空的推断，这就是解释消除（explaining away）。完整图为$B\to G\leftarrow F$、$G\to D$，对应图11.32的$a,b,c,d$。观测碰撞点$G$的后代$D$，同样可以使原先独立的$B,F$变为条件相关。
