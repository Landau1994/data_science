# 习题 PM004：三元二值分布的有向链分解

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **11.4 (★★)** Evaluate the distributions $p(a)$, $p(b\mid c)$, and $p(c\mid a)$ corresponding to the joint distribution given in Table 11.1. Hence, show by direct evaluation that $p(a,b,c)=p(a)p(c\mid a)p(b\mid c)$. Draw the corresponding directed graph.
>
> **Table 11.1** The joint distribution over three binary variables.
>
> | $a$ | $b$ | $c$ | $p(a,b,c)$ |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0.192 |
> | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0.144 |
> | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0.048 |
> | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0.216 |
> | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0.192 |
> | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0.064 |
> | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0.048 |
> | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0.096 |

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> 对表11.1的联合分布计算$p(a)$、$p(b\mid c)$、$p(c\mid a)$，据此直接验证$p(a,b,c)=p(a)p(c\mid a)p(b\mid c)$，并画出对应的有向图。
>
> 表11.1如下。
>
> | $a$ | $b$ | $c$ | $p(a,b,c)$ |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0.192 |
> | 0 | 0 | 1 | 0.144 |
> | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0.048 |
> | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0.216 |
> | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0.192 |
> | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0.064 |
> | 1 | 1 | 0 | 0.048 |
> | 1 | 1 | 1 | 0.096 |

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示 1
> 先按$a$与$c$求边缘，再使用条件概率的比值定义。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **独立推导。**
>
> 所需分布为
> $$
> p(a=0)=0.6,\quad p(a=1)=0.4,
> $$
> $$
> \begin{array}{c|cc}
> &b=0&b=1\\\hline
> c=0&0.8&0.2\\
> c=1&0.4&0.6
> \end{array}\qquad
> \begin{array}{c|cc}
> &c=0&c=1\\\hline
> a=0&0.4&0.6\\
> a=1&0.6&0.4
> \end{array}.
> $$
> 例如$p(c=0\mid a=0)=(0.192+0.048)/0.6=0.4$；其余各项同样由联合表求和后相除得到。按原表的八行顺序，三个因子的乘积依次为
> $$
> (0.6\cdot0.4\cdot0.8,\ 0.6\cdot0.6\cdot0.4,\ 0.6\cdot0.4\cdot0.2,\ 0.6\cdot0.6\cdot0.6,
> $$
> $$
> 0.4\cdot0.6\cdot0.8,\ 0.4\cdot0.4\cdot0.4,\ 0.4\cdot0.6\cdot0.2,\ 0.4\cdot0.4\cdot0.6)
> $$
> $$
> =(0.192,0.144,0.048,0.216,0.192,0.064,0.048,0.096),
> $$
> 与原表全部相同。相应的有向无环图为：
>
> ```mermaid
> flowchart LR
>     a((a)) --> c((c)) --> b((b))
> ```
>
> 该图的局部分布正是$p(a)$、$p(c\mid a)$、$p(b\mid c)$。
