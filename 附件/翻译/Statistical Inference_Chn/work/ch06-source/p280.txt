                              G1 = {ga,c(x): −∞< a < ∞, c > 0},
     where ga,c(x1,...,xn) = (cx1 +a,...,cxn +a), and
                                  G2 = {ga(x): −∞< a < ∞},
     where ga(x1,...,xn) = (x1 +a,...,xn +a).
       (a) Show that estimators of the form

                                          W(x1,...,xn) = ¯x+k,

        where k is a nonzero constant, are equivariant with respect to the group G2 but are not equivariant
         with respect to the group G1.
      (b) For each group, under what conditions does an equivariant estimator W satisfy EθW = θ, that is, it
            is unbiased for estimating θ?
