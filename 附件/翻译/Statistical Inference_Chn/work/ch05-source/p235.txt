with the Poisson assumptions.

5.6.2   Indirect Methods

When no easily found direct transformation is available to generate the desired random variable, an
extremely powerful indirect method, the Accept/Reject Algorithm, can often provide a solution.
   The idea behind the Accept/Reject Algorithm is, perhaps, best explained through a simple
example.
