   One method of proceeding is based on using a Taylor series approximation, which allows us to
approximate the mean and variance of a function of a random variable. We will also see that these
rather straightforward approximations are good enough to obtain a CLT. We begin with a short
review of Taylor series.

   For the statistical application of Taylor’s Theorem, we are most concerned with the first-order
Taylor series, that is, an approximation using just the first derivative (taking r = 1 in the above
formulas). Furthermore, we will also find use for a multivariate Taylor series. Since the above detail
is univariate, some of the following will have to be accepted on faith.
