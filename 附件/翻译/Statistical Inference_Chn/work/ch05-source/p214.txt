   The F distribution can be derived in a more general setting than is done here. A variance ratio
may have an F distribution even if the parent populations are not normal. Kelker (1970) has shown
that as long as the parent populations have a certain type of symmetry (spherical symmetry), then
the variance ratio will have an F distribution.
