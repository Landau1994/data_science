 5.8.2 The Bias of S2
      Most of the calculations that we have done in this chapter have assumed that the observations
       are independent, and calculations of some expectations have relied on this fact. H. A. David
       (1985) pointed out that, if the observations are dependent, then S2 may be a biased estimate
       of σ2. That is, it may not happen that ES2 = σ2. However, the range of the possible bias
         is easily calculated. If X1,...,Xn are random variables (not necessarily independent) with
