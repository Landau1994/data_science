   Although the formula for the bivariate normal pdf looks formidable, this bivariate distribution
is one of the most frequently used. (In fact, the derivation of the formula need not be formidable at
all. See Exercise 4.46.)
   The many nice properties of this distribution include these:
a. The marginal distribution of X is n(µX,σ2X).
b. The marginal distribution of Y is n(µY,σ2Y ).
c. The correlation between X and Y is ρXY = ρ.
d. For any constants a and b, the distribution of aX + bY  is n(aµX + bµY,a2σ2X + b2σ2Y +
  2abρσXσY).
