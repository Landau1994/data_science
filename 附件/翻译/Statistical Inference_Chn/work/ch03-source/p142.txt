3.8  Miscellanea

 3.8.1 The Poisson postulates
      The Poisson distribution can be derived from a set of basic assumptions, sometimes called
       the Poisson postulates. These assumptions relate to the physical properties of the process
       under consideration. While, generally speaking, the assumptions are not very easy to verify,
       they do provide an experimenter with a set of guidelines for considering whether the Poisson
        will provide a reasonable model. For a more complete treatment of the Poisson postulates,
       see the classic texts by Feller (1968) or Barr and Zehna (1983).
