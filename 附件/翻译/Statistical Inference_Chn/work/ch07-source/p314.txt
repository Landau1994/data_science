     It would be an atypical decision theoretic analysis in which the set D of allowable estimators was
restricted to the set of unbiased estimators, as was done in Section 7.3.2. Then, minimizing the risk
would just be minimizing the variance. A decision theoretic analysis would be more comprehensive
in that both the variance and bias are in the risk and will be considered simultaneously. An estimator
would be judged good if it had a small, but probably nonzero, bias combined with a small variance.
