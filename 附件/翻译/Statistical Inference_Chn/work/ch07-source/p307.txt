   The conditions for attainment of the Cram´er–Rao Lower Bound are actually quite simple. Recall
that the bound follows from an application of the Cauchy–Schwarz Inequality, so conditions for
attainment of the bound are the conditions for equality in the Cauchy–Schwarz Inequality (see
Section 4.7). Note also that Corollary 7.3.15 is a useful tool because it implicitly gives us a way of
finding a best unbiased estimator.
