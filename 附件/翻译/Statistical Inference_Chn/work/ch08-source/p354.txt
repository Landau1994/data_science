Example 8.3.30 (Fisher’s Exact Test)   Let S1 and S2 be independent observations with S1 ∼
binomial(n1, p1) and S2 ∼binomial(n2, p2). Consider testing H0 : p1 = p2 versus H1 : p1 > p2.Under H0, letting p denote the common value of p1 = p2, the joint pmf of (S1,S2) is

                                   n1                   n2
                   f(s1,s2|p) =     s1  ps1(1−p)n1−s1   s2  ps2(1−p)n2−s2
                                   n1    n2
                =                                        s1     s2   ps1+s2(1−p)n1+n2−(s1+s2).
