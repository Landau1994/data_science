8.3.3   Sizes of Union–Intersection and Intersection–Union Tests

Because of the simple way in which they are constructed, the sizes of union–intersection (UIT) and
intersection–union (IUT) tests can often be bounded above by the sizes of some other tests. Such
bounds are useful if a level α test is wanted, but the size of the UIT or IUT is too difficult to evaluate.
