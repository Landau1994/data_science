# 习题 P049：配方积分求线性高斯模型的边缘

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **3.26 (★★★)** This exercise and the next provide practice at manipulating the quadratic forms that arise in linear-Gaussian models, and they also serve as an independent check of results derived in the main text. Consider a joint distribution $p(\mathbf x,\mathbf y)$ defined by the marginal and conditional distributions given by (3.83) and (3.84). By examining the quadratic form in the exponent of the joint distribution and using the technique of ‘completing the square’ discussed in Section 3.2, find expressions for the mean and covariance of the marginal distribution $p(\mathbf y)$ in which the variable $\mathbf x$ has been integrated out. To do this, make use of the Woodbury matrix inversion formula (3.210). Verify that these results agree with (3.93) and (3.94).

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> **原题3.26。** 对式(3.83)、(3.84)的线性高斯联合指数配方，并用Woodbury公式积分掉 $\mathbf x$，独立验证边缘均值、协方差(3.93)–(3.94)。

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示
> 中心化 $\mathbf v=\mathbf x-\boldsymbol\mu$、$\mathbf d=\mathbf y-A\boldsymbol\mu-\mathbf b$，关于 $\mathbf v$ 配方。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **证明范围：独立推导。** 设 $\Lambda,L\succ0$，模型为 $\mathbf x\sim\mathcal N(\boldsymbol\mu,\Lambda^{-1})$、$\mathbf y\mid\mathbf x\sim\mathcal N(A\mathbf x+\mathbf b,L^{-1})$。令 $C=\Lambda+A^{\mathsf T}LA\succ0$，中心化后指数中的二次型为
> $$
> \begin{aligned}
> Q&=\mathbf v^{\mathsf T}\Lambda\mathbf v+(\mathbf d-A\mathbf v)^{\mathsf T}L(\mathbf d-A\mathbf v)\\
> &=(\mathbf v-C^{-1}A^{\mathsf T}L\mathbf d)^{\mathsf T}C(\mathbf v-C^{-1}A^{\mathsf T}L\mathbf d)
> +\mathbf d^{\mathsf T}[L-LAC^{-1}A^{\mathsf T}L]\mathbf d.
> \end{aligned}
> $$
> 积分第一项产生与 $\mathbf d$ 无关的因子 $(2\pi)^{\dim(\mathbf x)/2}|C|^{-1/2}$，留下高斯指数。Woodbury公式给出
> $$
> L-LA(\Lambda+A^{\mathsf T}LA)^{-1}A^{\mathsf T}L=(L^{-1}+A\Lambda^{-1}A^{\mathsf T})^{-1}.
> $$
> 这是正定矩阵；积分后的函数已由原联合密度保证归一化，因此它唯一对应
> $$
> p(\mathbf y)=\mathcal N(\mathbf y\mid A\boldsymbol\mu+\mathbf b,\ L^{-1}+A\Lambda^{-1}A^{\mathsf T}),
> $$
> 均值和协方差正是(3.93)–(3.94)。这里通过实际配方消去变量，而非只引用边缘公式。
