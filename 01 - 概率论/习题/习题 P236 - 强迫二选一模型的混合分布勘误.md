# 习题 P236：强迫二选一模型的混合分布勘误

## 题目

### 英文原题

> [!question] 英文原题
> **4.37** A generalization of the hierarchy in Exercise 4.34 is described by D. G. Morrison (1978), who gives a model for forced binary choices. A forced binary choice occurs when a person is forced to choose between two alternatives, as in a taste test. It may be that a person cannot actually discriminate between the two choices (can you tell Coke from Pepsi?), but the setup of the experiment is such that a choice must be made. Therefore, there is a confounding between discriminating correctly and guessing correctly. Morrison modeled this by defining the following parameters:
> $$
> p=\text{probability that a person can actually discriminate},\qquad c=\text{probability that a person discriminates correctly}.
> $$
> Then
> $$
> c=p+\tfrac12(1-p)=\tfrac12(1+p),\qquad\tfrac12<c<1,
> $$
> where $\tfrac12(1-p)$ is the probability that a person guesses correctly. We now run the experiment and observe $X_1,\ldots,X_n\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(c)$, so
> $$
> P(\Sigma X_i=k\mid c)=\binom nkc^k(1-c)^{n-k}.
> $$
> However, it is probably the case that $p$ is not constant from person to person, so $p$ is allowed to vary according to a beta distribution,
> $$P\sim\operatorname{beta}(a,b).$$
> (a) Show that the distribution of $\Sigma X_i$ is beta-binomial.
> (b) Find the mean and variance of $\Sigma X_i$.

### 中文转述

> [!question] 中文转述
> **原题4.37。**
> D. G. Morrison (1978) 描述了习题4.34 中层次结构的一个推广，建立了强迫二元选择（forced binary choice）模型. 强迫二元选择是指要求受试者必须在两个选项之间作出选择，例如品尝测试. 受试者可能实际上无法分辨两种选项（你能分清可口可乐与百事可乐吗？），但实验设计要求必须作出选择. 因此，正确辨别与正确猜中混杂在一起. Morrison 为此定义参数
>
> $$
> \begin{aligned}p&=\text{受试者确实能够分辨的概率},\\c&=\text{受试者判断正确的概率}.\end{aligned}
> $$
>
> 于是
>
> $$
> c=p+\frac12(1-p)=\frac12(1+p),\qquad\frac12<c<1,
> $$
>
> 其中 $\frac12(1-p)$ 是受试者猜对的概率. 进行实验并观测 $X_1,\ldots,X_n\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(c)$，所以
>
> $$
> P\left(\sum X_i=k\mid c\right)=\binom nk c^k(1-c)^{n-k}.
> $$
>
> 然而，$p$ 很可能因人而异，因此允许 $p$ 按 Beta 分布变化：$P\sim\operatorname{beta}(a,b)$.
>
> (a)  证明 $\sum X_i$ 的分布为 Beta–二项分布.
>
> (b)  求 $\sum X_i$ 的均值和方差.

## 提示

> [!hint]- 提示
> 混合的成功概率是$C=(1+P)/2$，不是原来的$P$。

## 解答

> [!success]- 解答
> **证明范围：独立推导。** 令$S=\sum_iX_i$；按题内二项式条件pmf，给定$P$后$n$次试验独立。参数$a,b>0$。
> (a) $C$支持在$(1/2,1)$，通常不是$(0,1)$上的Beta变量，故原结论若指通常Beta二项族不能成立。直接积分给完整pmf：
> $$
> P(S=k)=\frac{\binom nk}{2^nB(a,b)}\int_0^1(1+p)^kp^{a-1}(1-p)^{b+n-k-1}dp
> =\frac{\binom nk}{2^nB(a,b)}\sum_{j=0}^k\binom kjB(a+j,b+n-k),
> $$
> $k=0,\ldots,n$。这明确是平移缩放Beta成功概率的混合，非标准Beta二项。严格排除整个标准Beta二项族的反例可取$a=b=1,n=3$，此时$C\sim U(1/2,1)$。$ES/3=EC=3/4$且$E[S(S-1)]/6=EC^2=7/12$；若存在标准Beta混合参数$u,v$，这两矩强迫$u/(u+v)=3/4$、$\operatorname{Var}C=1/48$，解得$u=6,v=2$。但本模型$P(S=3)=EC^3=15/32$，Beta$(6,2)$混合却给$6\cdot7\cdot8/(8\cdot9\cdot10)=7/15$，两者不同。因此不仅原参数$a,b$不适用，某些$n$下也根本不存在任何标准Beta二项参数。
> (b) 令$m=EC=(2a+b)/[2(a+b)]$，$v=\operatorname{Var}C=ab/[4(a+b)^2(a+b+1)]$。全期望与全方差给$ES=nm$、$\operatorname{Var}S=nm(1-m)+n(n-1)v$。若原作者把该推广也称作Beta二项，应另核原论文；本解不据未读论文改变标准族的数学定义。
